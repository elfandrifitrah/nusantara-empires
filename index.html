Nusantara Empires

A real-time strategy game of the kingdoms of the Indonesian archipelago. Gather rice, wood, gold and stone. Build a court. Train an army. Hold the trade routes.

The 3D map. Hills, rivers, stone temples and faction banners. Drag in a match to pan. Use the wheel to zoom. Press Q and E to turn the view.

Eight factions stand ready. Each mission follows a real event. Notes mark each fact that sources dispute. You can win in play. History keeps its own end.

The eight factions

The arc of the game

Four ages

Age I is the Village Age. Age II is the Temple Age. Age III is the Kingdom Age. Age IV is the Empire Age. Each age opens new buildings and new units.

Five beats in each match

Gather. Build. Train. Fight or trade. Advance the age. The loop is short. The loop is the game.

Europe enters late

The Portuguese reach Malacca in 1511. The Dutch form the VOC in 1602. The English trade from Bantam. Late missions put these powers on the map.

Text uses ASD-STE100. Sentences stay short. Words stay simple. The codex lists the source for each entry. Disputed facts carry a red Disputed chip.

Source code and design plan: github.com/elfandrifitrah. The stack is free. The game runs in a web browser on Mac and PC. After the first load, single player and the codex work offline.

    How to play

    Select a unitClick the unit. Drag a box to select a group.
    Give an orderRight-click the target. A right-click on a tree, a mine or a field sends a villager to gather. A right-click on a foe sends soldiers to attack.
    Touch orderTap a unit to select it. Then tap the target. The game reads the second tap as the order.
    BuildSelect a villager. Click a building in the command grid. Click the map to place it.
    TrainSelect the Kraton or the Barracks. Click the unit in the command grid.
    Move the cameraUse W A S D or the arrow keys. Move the mouse to the screen edge. Or click the minimap.
    Zoom and turnUse the mouse wheel to zoom. Press Q and E to turn the view. Drag with the middle mouse button to turn. The buttons under the minimap do the same acts.
    Next step hintA gold bar at the foot of the map states the next step. Mission 1 adds a step list at the top right of the map.
    Place a buildingThe ghost shows the building place. Green means the place fits. Red means the place is blocked.
    HotkeysH selects the Kraton. . selects an idle villager. Esc cancels or pauses. Space pauses.
    Advance the ageClick Advance age in the top bar. A new age needs rice and gold. A new age opens new buildings and units.

    The economy

    Villagers gather four goods. Rice comes from rice fields and farms. Wood comes from trees. Gold comes from gold mines. Stone comes from stone piles. Houses raise the people cap. The Kraton trains villagers. The Barracks trains soldiers. The Temple trains the war elephant. An age rite from Age III on needs a Temple. The Tower guards a place. The Market gives a slow gold income from trade.

    Win and lose

    Each mission states one clear goal. You lose if the enemy destroys your Kraton. In a skirmish, you win if you destroy the enemy Kraton.
