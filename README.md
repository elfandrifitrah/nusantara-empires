# Nusantara Empires

Nusantara Empires is a real time strategy game for the browser.
The game covers the kingdoms of the Indonesian archipelago.
You gather rice, wood, gold, and stone.
You build a court.
You train an army.
You hold the trade routes.

Play the game here: https://elfandrifitrah.github.io/nusantara-empires/

## How to play

Start with Play campaign and pick a mission.
Or pick Play skirmish and choose one faction.
Click a villager to select it.
Right click a tree, a mine, or a field to gather.
Right click open ground to move.
Right click a foe to attack.
Drag a box to select a group.
Select a villager, click a building in the command grid, then click the map to place it.
Select the Kraton or the Barracks to train units.
Build Houses to raise the people cap.
Click Advance age in the top bar when you can afford it.
A new age opens new buildings and new units.
Move the camera with W, A, S, D, or the arrow keys.
Click the minimap to jump to a place.
Press H to jump to the Kraton.
Press the dot key to find an idle villager.
Press Space or Esc to pause.
You lose if the enemy destroys your Kraton.
In a skirmish, you win if you destroy the enemy Kraton.
Each mission states its own goal in the brief.

## Factions

The game has eight factions.
Srivijaya holds the strait trade.
Mataram Medang builds temples.
Majapahit fields the army of Gajah Mada.
Sunda grows rice in the wet fields of the west.
Demak grows fast in the coast towns.
Aceh fields archers and gunners.
Banten earns gold from the pepper trade.
Gowa-Tallo fields war elephants.

## Missions

The campaign has eight missions.
Each mission is one real event.
Mission 1 is Lord of the Strait, c. 700.
Mission 2 is Raise Borobudur, c. 825.
Mission 3 is The Mongol Trap, 1293.
Mission 4 is The Field of Bubat, 1357.
Mission 5 is The New Sultanate, c. 1500.
Mission 6 is Pepper and Guns, 1629.
Mission 7 is The Pepper Port, c. 1650.
Mission 8 is War for Makassar, 1666 to 1669.

## Codex

The codex has 20 entries.
The entries cover kingdoms, temples, scripts, and trade goods.
The entries cover the Portuguese arrival, the VOC, and the English.
Facts that sources dispute carry a Disputed chip.
Each entry names its sources.
You can win in play.
History keeps its own end.

## Tech

The game uses Three.js for the 3D map.
The game uses plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
The build uses free tools only.
The cost is zero.
